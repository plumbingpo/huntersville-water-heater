Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Huntersville Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan service around your operating hours.

Hot water temperature is a core issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water whenever the layout allows.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect flue draft and vent connections, confirm proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast for emergency repairs throughout Huntersville and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.
